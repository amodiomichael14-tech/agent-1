# Cold email instructions

This file controls how your cold emails are written. **Edit it any time** — the
next run uses whatever is here. Write it like you're briefing a smart assistant
who will write each email by hand. (The `<!-- ... -->` lines are just hints to
you and are ignored.)

## Who I am / what I'm offering
<!-- Replace this with your real details. -->
I'm a freelance web developer who builds fast, modern websites for small
businesses. I help owners who are stuck with a slow or outdated site get
something that actually brings in customers.

## Goal of the email
Start a conversation and get a reply. Ask if they'd be open to a quick
15-minute call this week — no pressure.

## Tone & style
- Warm, human, and concise — like a real person, not a marketer.
- 3 short paragraphs maximum. No buzzwords, no hype.
- Lead with something relevant to them, not a wall of text about me.
- One clear, low-pressure call to action at the end.

## Rules
- Never make up facts about the recipient or their business.
- Don't use "Dear" or stiff, formal language.
- No links or attachments unless I add them here.
